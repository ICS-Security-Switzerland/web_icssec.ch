{%- comment -%} The invitation to propose a talk, between the upcoming events and the archive. include.lang 'en' | 'de' | 'fr' | 'it' Text and guidelines come from _data/talks.yml. The address is the same in every language, so it lives here, as it does in contact-cards.html. A mailto link rather than a form: there is nothing to host, and a proposal turns into a conversation anyway. Subject and a short template are filled in, so that a proposal arrives with what is needed to judge it. Encoded like the calendar link in event-item.html (url_encode writes a space as "+", which is a literal plus in a URI), and with CRLF line breaks, which is what RFC 6068 asks for in a mailto body. The bare address stays visible next to the button for anyone whose browser has no mail client to hand the link to. The guidelines sit in a
: published and one click away, without turning an invitation into a wall of rules. The text fields go through markdownify, as the event fields do: links and emphasis work in the data, and apostrophes come out typographic, like those in the Markdown around the box. The wrapping
