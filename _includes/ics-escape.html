{%- comment -%} Turns one field from _data/events.yml into an RFC 5545 TEXT value. include.text the raw value, which may contain inline Markdown Two things have to happen and the order matters. Markdown first: the data carries links and emphasis meant for the website ("[Il Fermento](https://...)", "_(tbd)_"). A calendar entry has nowhere to put a link, so the markup is rendered and then stripped down to its text. Escaping second, per RFC 5545 section 3.3.11: backslash, semicolon, comma and newline are the four characters a TEXT value cannot carry raw. Backslash goes first — escaping it after the others would double the backslashes they just added. This is not cosmetic: "Detecon (Schweiz) AG, Loewenstrasse 1, 8001 Zurich" contains two commas, and an unescaped comma splits one LOCATION into three values. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign v = include.text | markdownify | strip_html | strip -%} {%- comment -%} markdownify emits entities; a calendar wants the characters. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign v = v | replace: '&', '&' | replace: '<', '<' | replace: '>', '>' | replace: '"', '"' | replace: ''', "'" | replace: '’', "'" -%} {%- assign v = v | replace: '\', '\\' -%} {%- assign v = v | replace: ';', '\;' | replace: ',', '\,' -%} {%- comment -%} Real newlines become the two-character sequence \n. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign v = v | newline_to_br | replace: '
