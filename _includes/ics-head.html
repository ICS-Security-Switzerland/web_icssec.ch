{%- comment -%} The VCALENDAR opening and the timezone definition for the per-event download. include.lang 'en' | 'de' | 'fr' | 'it' (used for the PRODID language tag) It is a separate include rather than being inlined into event-item.html so that the markup of a card stays readable next to the calendar plumbing, and so a second consumer — a whole-series file, say — could be added without the timezone rules being written out a second time and drifting. Times are written with TZID rather than converted to UTC, and the rules below are the EU daylight-saving rules, so a client resolves CET and CEST itself instead of guessing. No X-WR-CALNAME or X-WR-TIMEZONE. Both are non-standard hints that tell a client what to call a subscribed calendar and which zone to assume for it. There is no subscription here, and every time carries an explicit TZID backed by the VTIMEZONE below, so neither would do anything. {%- endcomment -%} BEGIN:VCALENDAR VERSION:2.0 PRODID:-//ICS Security Switzerland//icssec.ch//{{ include.lang | upcase }} CALSCALE:GREGORIAN METHOD:PUBLISH BEGIN:VTIMEZONE TZID:Europe/Zurich BEGIN:STANDARD DTSTART:19701025T030000 TZOFFSETFROM:+0200 TZOFFSETTO:+0100 TZNAME:CET RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY;BYMONTH=10;BYDAY=-1SU END:STANDARD BEGIN:DAYLIGHT DTSTART:19700329T020000 TZOFFSETFROM:+0100 TZOFFSETTO:+0200 TZNAME:CEST RRULE:FREQ=YEARLY;BYMONTH=3;BYDAY=-1SU END:DAYLIGHT END:VTIMEZONE
