{%- comment -%} The VCALENDAR opening and the timezone definition, shared by the subscribable calendar and by the single-event download on each card. include.lang 'en' | 'de' | 'fr' | 'it' It lives in its own file so the two callers cannot drift apart: a VTIMEZONE that differed between the series and a single download would put the same meetup at two different times in the same person's calendar.
