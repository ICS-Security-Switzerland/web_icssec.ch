## How it works

1. Fill in the form below and send it.
2. We look at your application personally and email you the association's statutes and your QR invoice.
3. Once the fee has arrived, you are a member.

<p class="join-note">You receive the statutes with our email. If you would like to read them before applying, or have any other question, write to <a href="mailto:info@icssec.ch">info@icssec.ch</a>.</p>
