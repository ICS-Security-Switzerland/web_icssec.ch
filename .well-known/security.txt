RFC 9116. Where to report a security problem with icssec.ch.

Expires is required and should be less than a year ahead. It is computed
from the build date (180 days ahead), and the site is rebuilt daily by the
workflow, so it never runs out while the site is maintained. If the daily
rebuild is ever switched off, this file goes stale after half a year —
which is the point of the field.
{%- endcomment -%}
